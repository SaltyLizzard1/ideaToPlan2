### How the price-validation protections are kept

1. **The financial prompt rule** (RC1d): a level reached never validates the price or demand.
2. **Compute Financials** (RC1d): any model sentence tying validation of price or demand to reaching a level, target, scenario or the forecast is left out of the notes and reported for your review. Sentences about customers who actually pay, negated sentences, and levels that only trigger spending are kept.
3. **Citation Check** (RC1d): the same claim in the writer's own prose is a finding.
4. **The fixed table wording:** the price row is labelled an untested scenario assumption, and "No result computed from it shows that customers will pay it."
5. **Fix 1:** whatever model text remains sits outside the locked tables, so it can be seen and corrected.

The 63222 answer, which carries the original defect, stays as the labelled historical fixture for these tests.
