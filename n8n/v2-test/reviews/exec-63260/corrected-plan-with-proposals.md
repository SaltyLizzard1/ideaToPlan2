## 2. Business Overview

QYLAT is a digital lifestyle-planning and consulting business. You describe it as a platform that combines real-world travel content with practical planning resources, including travel and relocation guides, interactive assessment tools, financial planning tools, skill and remote-work assessments, one-on-one planning sessions, and business planning services for people interested in creating location-independent income. The content side builds awareness through social media, travel stories, destination information, and firsthand experiences. Visitors are then directed to free planning tools that help identify their biggest barriers and next steps, with paid consulting as the primary revenue source.

Your target customer is an adult, typically aged 40 to 60, who is financially stable enough to consider a major lifestyle change but feels uncertain about how to make it happen. They are not looking for travel inspiration; they want practical guidance and a realistic plan.

**What is known and what is not.** Business stage, current revenue, years in operation, and what is already in place were not captured in the intake. This matters because the plan's starting point, the validation still needed, and the budget recommendations all depend on whether you already have a website, an audience, paying customers, or published content. The plan is written as a working assumption for a business that has not yet made its first sale in this specific offer. If you already have paying customers or a built audience, several early-stage steps below can be skipped, and the financial forecast should be revised upward.

**The stated differentiator.** You described your differentiator as "experience." That is not yet specific enough to use in positioning or sales conversations. Before you can test whether it matters to buyers, you need to write down exactly what experience you have: which countries you have lived in or traveled to, what life changes you have made yourself, what results you have helped others achieve, and what you know that a customer could not easily find elsewhere. An early action in the 90-Day Plan addresses this directly.

**The platform vision versus the first revenue step.** The full QYLAT vision, a content platform with interactive tools, affiliate partnerships, and multiple revenue streams, is a longer build than the first revenue step. The financial model is built around the first revenue-generating piece: paid one-on-one planning sessions. IdeaToPlan recommends treating the platform vision as a direction and the planning session as the thing you validate and sell first, because it requires the smallest audience to generate income and can be tested through direct outreach before any infrastructure is built.
